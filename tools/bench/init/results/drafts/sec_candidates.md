## 3. The candidates, as built for this test

Every candidate got the same six stand-in services (section 4.2) and, where the init could declare it, the same facts: the dependency order (driftwm needs seatd and udevd; Waybar needs driftwm and dbus; hubd needs driftwm), readiness, logging into a capped log, and the resource policy for driftwm and hubd (OOM score -900, nice -5, own cgroup v2 group). Each candidate's files are in `tools/bench/init/candidates/NAME/`. Where an init has a native way to say something, it was used; where it has none, the shell scripts of today's image were used (including today's polling script `follow-driftwm`). Versions are those actually run.

**The watchdog guard** (`hubsim guard`, about 100 lines of Go) runs as an ordinary service of every candidate (outside OpenRC's supervision for OpenRC). It feeds `/dev/watchdog` (QEMU's i6300esb, 30 s timeout) while (a) it is scheduled and (b) PID 1 is not stopped or traced and still reaps an orphaned zombie within 3 s. It never looks at any service. After three failed checks in a row (about 9 s) it stops feeding, and the hardware resets the machine. The existing image's feeder is `busybox watchdog -F -t 5 -T N /dev/watchdog` (SOURCE: `image/rootfs/etc/s6/sv/wd/run`, read 2026-10-05): it is also independent of services but does not look at PID 1.
